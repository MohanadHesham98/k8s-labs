Daily Practices

Wellness Tips for Everyday

Small, intentional habits that nurture your mental health over time.

Practice Deep Breathing

Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 6. Just 5 minutes can calm your nervous system.

Gratitude Journaling

Write down three things you're grateful for each evening. It rewires your brain to notice the good.

5-Minute Body Scan

Slowly move attention from toes to head, noticing sensations without judgment. A grounding reset.

Screen-Free Hour

Power down devices an hour before bed. Better sleep means better mental resilience tomorrow.

Connect with Someone

Reach out to a friend or family member daily. Social connection is a core emotional need.

Move Your Body

A 20-minute walk releases endorphins. Movement is medicine for both body and mind.
